What I learned + what comes next

Credibility comes from the decision process

A credible AI planning assistant is more than a good answer.Evidence is inspectable.
Uncertainty is actionable.
Human decisions are explicit.

An independent academic prototype.
No museum endorsement, deployment or measured classroom learning impact.

Test with actual educators

Include K–12 teachers and museum stakeholders. Measure end-to-end usefulness.

Guide curriculum input

Offer selectable subjects and topics alongside free-text goals.

Make instruction more specific

Customize the detail and activities in the learning journey.

Broaden and refresh the evidence

Expand source coverage and strengthen factual and safety checks.

Assess operational feasibility

Verify full-workflow cost, deployment needs and organizational readiness.
