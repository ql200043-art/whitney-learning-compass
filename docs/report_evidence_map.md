## P01 Impact + Metrics addendum — October 23, 2026

The [complete ten-section P01 report](impact_study_results.md) is the canonical human-study record. It documents one genuine Yale School of Management graduate-student **proxy user**, with no K–12 teaching experience, using the official catalog first and Compass second for the same Grade 4, 24-student, $400 assumed-USD, 90-minute force/motion/design scenario. Both tasks included identifying a suitable program, calculating/comparing the class fee, and considering unresolved booking/capacity information. The catalog choice was Simple Machines; the Compass choice was Marble Roller Coaster. Both met the applicable verified constraints at $336 and 90 minutes, without confirming a booking.

Observed active selection time was **135 seconds versus 65 seconds**, a **70-second (51.85%) descriptive reduction for this participant**. Both times exclude AI response waiting, learning-plan review and feedback completion. This is not an end-to-end elapsed-time saving or a causal estimate: n = 1, fixed order and prior task exposure limit interpretation. No student-learning, classroom-delivery or museum-adoption outcome was measured.

Participant perceptions include greater ease of discovery/comparison (catalog **3/5**, Compass **4/5**), useful integration of fees/time/unknowns, and convenient inquiry drafting. Critical feedback describes the learning plan as generic and open-ended goal entry as difficult compared with predefined filters. These are paraphrased perceptions, not independently measured reductions in page visits or email-writing time. The report's labeled implications are researcher interpretations, not additional participant observations.

This addendum supersedes the evidence map's earlier “no participant yet” status only. Earlier protocol, validation receipts, manifests and technical results remain pre-P01 historical snapshots. P01 does not change the 24-scenario population or its 24/24 final result, retained harness failure, model experiments, token/cost calculations, agent traces or safety findings. Original screenshots/raw notes have not been independently inspected, precise timer events remain undocumented, and direct-quote permission is not established. Further user testing and any product changes remain proposed next steps, not actions performed here.
