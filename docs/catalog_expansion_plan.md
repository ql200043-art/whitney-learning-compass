## Intended research sequence, after user review

1. Recheck the five pilot records, especially Bridges' missing duration, any grade discrepancies and pricing freshness.
2. Research the 15 detail pages in small batches, rotating themes and grade bands. Use the existing dictionary and field-evidence structure. Preserve official labels even where two labels mean similar things.
3. Verify identity, grades, subjects, price basis, duration and any published program-specific standards. Record access failures and nulls. Do not infer a fee or session length from a neighboring program.
4. Check the combined catalog's coverage across Grades 2–7. Identify thin grade/theme combinations. Any substitution must be another real official entry, documented and reviewed, with the total held to 20.
5. Check unique IDs/URLs, conversions, evidence resolution and unknown-field completeness. Keep unresolved candidates out of confirmed-match claims.
6. Present the expanded catalog and unresolved questions for review before implementation work.
