## Definitions and uncertainty

- **Engineering:** Mechanisms, engineering or explicit problem-driven design; merely assembling an object is insufficient.
- **Sustainability and Energy:** Environmental systems, sustainable materials, adaptation or renewable/clean energy; generic mechanical energy transfer alone is excluded.
- **Science:** Explicit physical, natural, biological, earth or space science subjects or experiments.
- **Interdisciplinary Learning:** Explicit links across arts, mathematics, history, culture or social studies and another discipline; not every workshop is automatically classified.

Uncertain assignments excluded from counts: 0.
This means the recorded editorial assignments have supporting reviewed evidence, not that the categories are objective museum classifications.
